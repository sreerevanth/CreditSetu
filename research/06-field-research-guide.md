# 06 · Field research guide

The brief asks for field research. These scripts are for short conversations (15–20 minutes) with the three groups that matter most. Even 3–5 interviews before the final pitch make the story much stronger. Quote real people (with permission) rather than the persona.

**Before you start:** say who you are, that it's for a student/hackathon project, that you won't record names without permission, and that they can stop anytime. Don't ask for documents or account numbers.

## A. Micro-enterprise owners

**Business**
1. What does your business do, and for how long?
2. Roughly how much comes in during a good month? A slow month? Which months are slow?
3. How do customers usually pay you: cash, UPI, bank transfer?

**Credit history**
4. Have you ever taken a loan for the business? From where: bank, NBFC, SHG, moneylender, family?
5. What interest did you pay? How long did it take to get the money?
6. Have you ever tried a bank loan and stopped partway? What made you stop?

**Schemes**
7. Which government loan schemes have you heard of? (Don't prompt first. Then ask about MUDRA, PM Vishwakarma, PMEGP.)
8. Has anyone offered to "arrange" a loan for a fee? How much did they ask for?

**Documents and records**
9. Which of these do you have: Aadhaar, PAN, bank statement, Udyam registration, GST, ITR?
10. Do you keep any record of sales: a notebook, WhatsApp, an app?

**Trust and control**
11. Would you let an app read your bank statement to check which loans you qualify for? What would make you comfortable or uncomfortable?
12. Would you rather use WhatsApp, an app or a website? Which language?

## B. Bank branch managers / credit officers

1. For a micro loan under ₹5 lakh, what documents do you actually need?
2. What share of applications come in incomplete? What's most often missing?
3. How long does an incomplete file cost you, roughly?
4. How do you assess a borrower with no ITR? Do you look at bank statements, UPI history, GST?
5. Do you use CGTMSE or CGFMU cover? What stops you using it more?
6. If a file arrived complete, with a cash-flow summary and the scheme fit explained, would that change how quickly you process it?
7. What would you need to trust a summary prepared by an app?

## C. Business correspondents, agents and SHG federation staff

1. How many people ask you about loans each month? How many end up applying?
2. Which schemes do people ask about most, and which do they not know about?
3. How long does it take you to prepare one application?
4. What do informal agents charge, and what do they actually do for that fee?
5. Would a tool that prepares the file help you serve more people? What would it need to do?

## After each interview

Write down within an hour:
- Three things that surprised you
- One direct quote you'd use in the pitch (with permission)
- Any number they gave you (interest rate, time, fee, documents)

Add notes to `research/interviews/` as one file per interview, without names or identifying details.
